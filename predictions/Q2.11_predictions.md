# Q2.11 — Predictions before training models B, C and D

Written after Testing the Baseline (Tables 3–4, Figures 3–5) and before any of
models B, C or D was trained.

Model B (symmetry augmentation) will shrink C_M and C_R and bring E_M and E_R
down to about E(5), since the remaining mirror/rotation gap comes from digit
appearance, but it will not help at 2× or 3×. Model C (speed augmentation)
will fix most of the 2× failure, bringing E(2) at 2× well below Blank, because
6.6 px per frame is within model A's 8 px reach and was only unfamiliar; at 3×
(about 10 px, beyond the reach) it will improve only partly. Model D (larger
reach, also speed-augmented) will also fix 3×, with E(2) at 3× clearly below
model C's, while staying about as accurate as A at 1×.
